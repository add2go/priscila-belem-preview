BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few practical things.

Which languages do you create in?+

I create in English and Brazilian Portuguese. We can discuss the language and approach that fit your audience.

Can we work together from another country?+

Yes. I’m based in Canada and welcome international inquiries. On-location projects depend on the destination and travel availability.

Can my brand use the content in ads?+

Usage is agreed as part of each proposal. Let me know whether you need organic content, paid advertising assets or both.

Is posting on your profile included?+

Content creation and posting to my own channels are separate scopes. Include any posting needs in your inquiry so we can discuss them.
